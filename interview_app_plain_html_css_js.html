Purpose of the Guide

This interview guide is designed to obtain detailed information from Computer Science lecturers on the implementation of the Computer Science curriculum in Colleges of Education in Akwa Ibom State. It focuses on curriculum coverage, resource adequacy, practical teaching, assessment practices, implementation challenges, and student outcomes.

Introductory Statement: Thank you for agreeing to participate in this study. There are no right or wrong answers. I am interested in your honest views and experiences. With your permission, this interview will be recorded for transcription and analysis, and all information provided will be treated confidentially.
